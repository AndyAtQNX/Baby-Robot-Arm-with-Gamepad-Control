> @brief ROS2 node for controlling a 5-DOF robotic arm on QNX Neutrino RTOS.
> 
> This node manages all hardware-level servo control for a 5-DOF robotic arm
> (plus gripper) using joystick input and/or inverse kinematics (IK) commands.
> It subscribes to joystick messages and IK solver output, converts commands
> to PWM signals, and sends them to the servos via a PCA9685 PWM driver over I2C.
> 
> Hardware Configuration:
>     - Joints 0-2 (Base, Shoulder, Elbow): DS3218 servos, 270 degree mode
>       Pulse range: 500-2500us, Neutral: 1500us [DS3218 Datasheet]
>     - Joints 3-4 (Wrist Pitch, Wrist Roll): MG90S micro servos, 180 degree mode
>       Pulse range: 1000-2000us, Neutral: 1500us [MG90S Datasheet]
>     - Joint 5 (Gripper): MG90S micro servo, 180 degree mode
>       Pulse range: 1000-2000us, Neutral: 1500us [MG90S Datasheet]
>     - PWM Driver: PCA9685 16-channel 12-bit PWM controller over I2C at 50Hz
>
